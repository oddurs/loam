loam keeps a repository's written context — research, design, reference,
guides — as Markdown under a schema, the way cairn keeps its backlog. cairn
records why things changed; loam keeps how they are now.

**Reading this roadmap.** Milestones are in dependency order, each a claim the
program can honestly make once it lands. v0.0 builds nothing: it closes the
decisions and writes the specification, because the format is the part that is
expensive to change. `cairn list --view decide` shows what is waiting on a
person; `cairn next` shows what can start.
