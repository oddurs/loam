## Question

Must every page carry YAML frontmatter, or can loam read a plain Markdown file
and infer what it needs?

## Why it has to be answered first

It decides what adopting a folder costs. poptop's pages have no frontmatter at
all. code-as-color's carry status as a bold line in the body. If frontmatter is
required, adoption is a rewrite of every page — and a tool that asks for that on
day one is not adopted.

## Options

1. **Required.** Simplest reader, and a wall in front of every existing folder.
2. **Optional, with inference.** `title` from the first H1, `kind` from the
   directory, `status` defaults to current, `summary` from the first paragraph.
   Frontmatter overrides any of them.
3. **Never.** Everything inferred. Nowhere to put `covers` or `supersedes`.

## What would settle it

Run option 2's inference by hand over every page in the three real folders and
count how many come out right. If nearly all do, inference is the format; if
many need correcting, frontmatter is.

## Answer

**Optional, with inference (option 2).** A page needs no frontmatter. Its title
is its first level-1 heading, its summary the paragraph directly under that
heading, its kind the kind that claims its directory, and its status `current`.
Frontmatter overrides any of them. The rules are exact, not heuristic, and are
spec §5.

### The evidence

The inference was written as `spec/reader.py` and run over every page of the
three folders, at the commits in `spec/corpus/NOTICE.md`. None of the 69 files
has frontmatter. Each result was then judged by hand: would a person accept it
in an index?

| Folder | Pages | Title right | Summary right | Summary wrong | No summary |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| code-as-color | 17 | 16 | 6 | 6 | 5 |
| poptop | 37 | 37 | 24 | 3 | 10 |
| measure-of-the-world | 15 | 15 | 9 | 3 | 3 |
| **all** | **69** | **68** | **39** | **12** | **18** |

- **Title: 68 of 69.** The one miss, `roadmap-intro.md`, has no heading because
  it is not a page: it is the header `cairn render` puts on a roadmap (0060).
- **Summary: 39 right of the 51 produced.** The 12 wrong are five ADRs whose
  first paragraph is `**Status:** accepted` (moving to cairn, 0061), two
  "generated by" notices (0060), one agent prompt that opens "You are writing…",
  and four pages that open with a detail rather than a statement of purpose.
- **No summary: 18**, all pages that go from the title straight to a
  `##` section, a table or a code block. The first version of the rule looked
  further down the page for a paragraph and found worse sentences than none —
  "The last one matters on a Mac" under a code block — so only the block
  directly under the title counts.

So inference is the format. Adopting any of the three folders asks nothing of
it; getting a good index out of it asks a `summary:` line of about one page in
ten, and those are the pages whose first paragraph is not doing the job a first
paragraph should (spec §11).

Requiring frontmatter (option 1) would have meant 69 edits to adopt three
folders that already had a title on every page but one. Never allowing it
(option 3) would leave nowhere to say `status: draft` or `supersedes`, which no
amount of inference can find.
